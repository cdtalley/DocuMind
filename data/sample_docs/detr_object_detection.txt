End-to-End Object Detection with Transformers
Nicolas Carion et al.
2020
arXiv:2005.12872

Abstract
DETR reframes object detection as direct set prediction and removes hand-designed components such as non-maximum suppression.

Methodology
The approach uses a CNN backbone and transformer encoder-decoder with bipartite matching loss.
Training is longer than classical detectors but the architecture is simpler end-to-end.

Datasets
Experiments are reported on the COCO benchmark.

Results
DETR achieves competitive detection quality with a cleaner design and strong extensibility.
