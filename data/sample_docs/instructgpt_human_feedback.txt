Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback
Long Ouyang et al.
2022
arXiv:2203.02155

Abstract
InstructGPT aligns large language models to human intent using supervised fine-tuning on demonstrations followed by reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), reducing harmful or unhelpful outputs relative to base models.

Methodology
The pipeline stages are: (1) collect human-written demonstrations of desired behavior on prompts, (2) fine-tune the base LM supervisedly, (3) train a reward model from human rankings of model outputs, (4) optimize policy with PPO against the reward model with a KL penalty toward the supervised policy.

Datasets
Demonstrations and comparison labels are collected from held-out human labelers across diverse prompts intended to reflect real user requests. Helpfulness and honesty criteria are emphasized during ranking.

Experiments
Human side-by-side evaluation measures preference for InstructGPT outputs versus baselines. Automatic metrics include toxicity scores and truthfulness probes on selected benchmarks.

Results
Smaller Instruct-tuned models can be preferred over larger base models on human evaluations, illustrating alignment gains beyond raw scale.
