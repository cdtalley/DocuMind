An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale
Alexey Dosovitskiy, Lucas Beyer, Alexander Kolesnikov, Dirk Weissenborn, Xiaohua Zhai, Thomas Unterthiner, Mostafa Dehghani, Matthias Minderer, Georg Heigold, Sylvain Gelly, Jakob Uszkoreit, Neil Houlsby
2020
arXiv:2010.11929

Abstract
Vision Transformer (ViT) applies a pure Transformer encoder to sequences of image patches, matching or exceeding CNNs on large-scale supervised pretraining while simplifying inductive biases.

Methodology
Images are split into fixed-size patches linearly embedded with position embeddings. Class token aggregation follows BERT-style pooling. Training uses heavy data augmentation and AdamW with cosine learning rate schedules.

Datasets
Pretraining is performed on ImageNet-21k or JFT-300M in large-scale variants. Fine-tuning and evaluation report top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-1k and transfer to CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, Oxford-IIIT Pets, and VTAB suites.

Experiments
The paper studies patch size, model depth, pretraining dataset size, and compute budgets, showing ViT benefits disproportionately from scale versus inductive priors in CNNs.

Results
ViT establishes that Transformer architectures are competitive for vision when paired with sufficient data and regularization.
