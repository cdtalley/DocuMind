XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System
Tianqi Chen, Carlos Guestrin
2016

Abstract
XGBoost is a scalable gradient boosting framework designed for speed and model performance.

Methodology
The objective combines a differentiable loss and regularization term.
The paper describes second-order approximation, sparsity-aware split finding, and distributed training.

Datasets
The paper evaluates benchmark datasets from classification and ranking tasks including Higgs and Allstate.

Results
XGBoost consistently outperforms baseline tree boosting systems in training speed and predictive performance.
