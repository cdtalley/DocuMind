Wide & Deep Learning for Recommender Systems
Heng-Tze Cheng et al., Google
2016

Abstract
The Wide & Deep architecture jointly trains a shallow linear model with cross-product feature interactions (wide) alongside a deep neural network embedding tower (deep) for app recommendation CTR prediction.

Methodology
Wide component memorizes sparse co-occurrences while deep component generalizes through dense embeddings of categorical features. The two towers are jointly optimized with logistic loss on binary install labels.

Datasets
Production-scale logs include anonymized user events and app impressions from mobile app stores. Public experiments often cite MovieLens as an academic analogue for collaborative filtering baselines, though production scale differs.

Experiments
Offline metrics include AUC and calibration while online A/B tests measure install rate and long-term engagement.

Results
The hybrid model improved offline AUC and online conversion versus wide-only and deep-only variants in reported experiments.
