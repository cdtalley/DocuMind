Language Models are Few-Shot Learners
Tom B. Brown et al.
2020
arXiv:2005.14165

Abstract
This paper investigates scaling autoregressive language models and shows strong few-shot and zero-shot capabilities.

Methodology
The model family scales transformer parameter counts and training data size.
Evaluation is performed without gradient-based task-specific fine-tuning for many tasks.

Datasets
The study uses diverse NLP benchmark datasets and task prompts across translation, QA, and cloze tasks.

Results
Large models substantially improve few-shot performance but expose biases and evaluation robustness concerns.
