Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition
Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren, Jian Sun
2015
arXiv:1512.03385

Abstract
Residual connections enable substantially deeper neural networks by improving optimization and gradient flow.

Methodology
The paper introduces residual blocks with identity shortcuts.
Model families include 34, 50, 101, and 152-layer variants.

Datasets
Evaluation includes ImageNet and CIFAR-10 settings.

Results
Residual networks outperform prior architectures and become foundational in modern computer vision systems.
