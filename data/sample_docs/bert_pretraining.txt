BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding
Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang, Kenton Lee, Kristina Toutanova
2018
arXiv:1810.04805

Abstract
BERT proposes deep bidirectional Transformer pre-training with masked language modeling and next sentence prediction.

Methodology
Pre-training is performed on BooksCorpus and English Wikipedia.
Fine-tuning is task-specific with minimal architecture changes.

Evaluation
The paper reports strong performance on GLUE, MultiNLI, SQuAD v1.1, and SQuAD v2.0 benchmarks.
