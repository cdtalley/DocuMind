Attention Is All You Need
Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit
2017
arXiv:1706.03762

Abstract
This paper introduces the Transformer architecture based entirely on self-attention, removing recurrence and convolution.

Methodology
The model uses multi-head self-attention, position-wise feed-forward layers, residual connections, and layer normalization.
Training used Adam optimizer with warmup and inverse square root learning rate schedule.

Results
Transformer achieves state-of-the-art BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German and English-to-French translation tasks.
